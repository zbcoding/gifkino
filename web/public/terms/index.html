About

Gifkino is a free, open-source GIF editor made by one developer, zbcoding (“I”, “me”). This website has a landing page and a browser version of the editor at /edit/. It is hosted on the Internet Computer, a public network run by independent node providers. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT licence.

Privacy

No data about you is stored or collected.

  • Your files stay on your device. GIFs, videos and images you open are processed inside your browser. They are never uploaded to this website or anywhere else.
  • No accounts, cookies, analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Settings stay in your browser. The editor keeps your settings and keybindings in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. They never leave your device; clearing the site's data deletes them.

Some services outside my control see your requests when you use the site:

  • The Internet Computer's HTTP gateways deliver these pages and, like any web server, receive your IP address and the addresses you request. They are operated by the network, not by me.
  • jsDelivr or unpkg serve the video decoder (ffmpeg) the first time you import a video, after asking you.
  • GitHub and Tally receive what you send them if you follow the “Report an issue” or “Quick feedback form” links.

Each of these has its own privacy policy.
