About, terms and privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

About

Gifkino is a free, open-source GIF editor made by its developer(s). This website offers it to download and to use in the browser. It is hosted on the Internet Computer, a public network run by independent node providers. The source code is public.

Privacy

Your files and edits are never stored or collected.

  • Your files stay on your device. GIFs, videos and images you open are processed inside your browser. They are never uploaded to this website or anywhere else.
  • Settings stay in your browser. The editor keeps your preferences in your browser's storage so they survive a reload. They never leave your device; clearing the site's data deletes them.
  • Visits are counted with privacy-focused analytics, currently Cloudflare Web Analytics. When a page loads, it reports basic information about the visit, such as the page, the referring site, and your browser and device type, to the analytics provider, which also sees your IP address. The developer(s) see only totals, such as visits per page and per country. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies, does not fingerprint visitors and does not track them across sites; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Some services outside the developers' control see your requests when you use the site:

  • The network that hosts the site delivers these pages and, like any web server, receives your IP address and the addresses you request.
  • Content delivery networks serve some parts of the editor that are downloaded only when needed, such as the video decoder.
  • Sites the website links to, such as the issue tracker or the feedback form, receive what you send them if you follow those links.

Each of these has its own privacy policy.
